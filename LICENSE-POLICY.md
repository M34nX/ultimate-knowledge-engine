# Licence and Reuse Policy

## Current state

No project-wide open-source licence has been selected yet. Until a licence is added, do not assume that code, documentation, artwork, or other repository material may be copied, modified, redistributed, or used commercially.

A public GitHub repository is not automatically public domain and is not automatically open source.

## Decisions required

The project owner should select and document separate policies for:
- software and tooling;
- original educational prose and diagrams;
- code examples;
- third-party assets and excerpts;
- datasets and generated indexes.

A single licence may not suit every content type.

## Third-party resources

Before importing third-party material:
1. Identify the original author and canonical source.
2. Verify the exact licence and version.
3. Check attribution, share-alike, non-commercial, and no-derivatives conditions.
4. Record the source and required notices.
5. Prefer linking to the original when reuse rights are unclear.
6. Do not assume public visibility grants copying permission.

Record applicable notices in `LICENSES/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`. Do not add copied resources until reuse rights are verified.
