# Governance

## Current stage

UKE is currently an owner-led project. Roles and decision processes may evolve as the contributor community grows.

## Roles

- **Project owner:** repository administration, releases, and final project decisions.
- **Maintainers:** contribution reviews, issue triage, and standards enforcement.
- **Subject reviewers:** technical correctness within their expertise.
- **Language reviewers:** translation quality and terminology.
- **Contributors:** authoring, testing, translating, and improving content.

One person may hold multiple roles, but review claims must remain honest and traceable.

## Decision principles

1. Accuracy and learner safety take precedence over speed.
2. Reuse and licensing must be clear.
3. Content should be modular and maintainable.
4. Important architecture decisions should be recorded as ADRs.
5. Breaking structural changes require migration notes.
6. Disagreements should be resolved through evidence and documented reasoning.

High-impact changes to schemas, navigation, security, or licences should receive additional review. Releases must describe what is actually included and what remains incomplete.
