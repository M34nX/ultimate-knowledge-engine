# Projects & Research

**Domain ID:** 11  
**Status:** Planned content area

This domain turns knowledge into applied work and introduces disciplined technical research.

## Topic map

- Guided projects organised by skill and difficulty.
- Project briefs, requirements, milestones, and acceptance criteria.
- Case studies and architecture analysis.
- Reproducible experiments and benchmark methodology.
- Reading technical papers and evaluating evidence.
- Writing technical reports and documenting decisions.
- Portfolio quality, maintenance, and responsible collaboration.

## Project standard

Every project should state prerequisites, scope, deliverables, constraints, evaluation criteria, safety considerations, and opportunities for extension. Avoid projects that require paid services or personal data unless safe alternatives are provided.
