# AI & Data Science

**Domain ID:** 05  
**Status:** Planned content area

This domain covers data collection, analysis, statistical reasoning, machine learning, and responsible AI systems.

## Topic map

- Mathematics and statistics for data work.
- Data acquisition, cleaning, quality, and governance.
- Exploratory analysis and visualisation.
- Classical machine learning and model evaluation.
- Neural networks and deep learning.
- Natural language processing and computer vision.
- Data engineering and reproducible workflows.
- Responsible AI, privacy, bias, and evaluation.

## Teaching standard

Explain assumptions, data limitations, evaluation methodology, uncertainty, and failure modes. Do not present benchmark scores as proof of general capability.
