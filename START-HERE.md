# Start Here

Welcome to Ultimate Knowledge Engine.

## Choose your path

- **Learner:** explore roadmaps under `learning/roadmaps/` as they are authored.
- **Contributor:** read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), then use a template.
- **Technical reviewer:** check accuracy, sources, version scope, examples, and objectives.
- **Translator:** read [the translation standard](docs/standards/translation.md) and preserve the stable concept ID.
- **Maintainer:** review [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), schemas, and quality gates.

## How the knowledge model works

1. A **domain** groups a subject area.
2. A **language guide** describes one language or technology.
3. A **concept** is a reusable knowledge unit with a permanent ID.
4. A **lesson** teaches concepts toward measurable outcomes.
5. An **exercise** checks whether a learner can apply knowledge.
6. A **reference** records source provenance and reuse constraints.

These are related but distinct content types.

## Before adding a page

- Search for an existing concept ID and equivalent title.
- Decide whether the material belongs in the dictionary, a lesson, or a language guide.
- Define audience, prerequisites, and learning objectives.
- Cite authoritative sources.
- Include version scope where relevant.
- Keep new content in Draft until review is complete.

Registry inclusion means planned or tracked coverage, not publication or endorsement.
