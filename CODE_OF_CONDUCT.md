# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

The UKE community aims to be respectful, inclusive, constructive, and focused on learning. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or humiliation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain disagreements with reasons and sources.
- Welcome beginner questions and different backgrounds.
- Respect culture, accessibility needs, and privacy.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them transparently.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, targeted insults, intimidation, doxxing, sexual harassment, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers using a verified contact channel. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially where possible and take proportionate action, including warnings, content removal, restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where a participant represents the project.
